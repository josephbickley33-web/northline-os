The problem

Running a haulage business shouldn't take five systems and a spreadsheet.

Most UK operators piece the day together from telematics, tachograph downloads, a warehouse system, the accounts package, WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets. The transport manager carries the gaps in their head, and the operator licence carries the risk.

  • Drivers' hours problems are spotted after the shift, not before the job is planned.
  • MOT, PMI and service dates live in a spreadsheet that one person keeps up to date.
  • Walkaround defects arrive on paper or in a photo on someone's phone.
  • Stock runs out because nobody saw the trend until the pallet wasn't there.
  • "AI" tools promise automation, but nobody can say who approved what, or why.
Features

Everything the office and the yard need, in one place.

Each person sees what their role needs: directors, duty managers, planners, workshop, warehouse and drivers.

Fleet and compliance

Every vehicle with its status, MOT and service dates. Off-road and overdue vehicles are flagged and kept off the plan. Alerts 30 days before an MOT is due.

Drivers' hours guard

Daily and weekly driving time against GB rules, shown to planners and to the driver. The hours guard is a hard constraint: no forecast or confidence score can override it.

Stock and warehouse

Stock by depot and location, every movement recorded, and low-stock alerts at your reorder points. Import your current stock list from a spreadsheet.

AI forecasting, human approval

Models forecast ETAs, workshop risk, stock and margin, each with an honest uncertainty range. Proposals wait in a queue until a named person approves or rejects them.

Driver app

Walkaround checks with photos, defect reports straight to the workshop, and proof of delivery with the receiver's signature. Runs in the phone's browser, with nothing to install.

Staff board

Channels for night operations, each depot and safety, updating live. Replaces the WhatsApp groups and keeps the messages inside the company.

Admin and audit

Invite staff by link, set roles, brand the app with your logo and colour, and import vehicles, jobs and stock from CSV. An append-only audit log records who did what, and when.

Notifications

One bell for MOTs due within 30 days, low stock, late or at-risk jobs, open defects and approvals waiting. Each person only sees alerts for their own role.

Finance view

Revenue, cost and margin from your jobs, so you can see which work pays. Accounts integration is part of the Enterprise plan.

How the AI works safely

AI that proposes, explains and waits.

We built Northline on the view that a model should never be the last word on safety, the law or a customer promise.

Northline supports decisions. It does not replace the operator licence holder, the transport manager or anyone's legal duties.

Forecasts come with an honest range

Instead of a single number, each forecast shows a range it expects to hit 90% of the time (conformal prediction), plus a calibrated confidence score.

The law is a hard limit, not a suggestion

GB drivers' hours and vehicle roadworthiness are fixed constraints. The legal hours guard is locked on and cannot be switched off, even by an admin.

New models start in shadow mode

A model runs alongside your team without acting, so you can compare its calls with reality before anyone switches it on.

A named person approves

Proposals sit in an approval queue. Approving or rejecting records the person, the time, the confidence and their note in an append-only audit log.

Security and UK GDPR

Your data stays yours, and stays in the UK.

Each customer gets a private workspace. Company data is separated at the database level, not just hidden in the screens.

Hosted in London

The database and file storage run in the London region (eu-west-2) of our hosting provider, Supabase, on Amazon Web Services.

Separated by company

Row-level security in the database means one company's users can never read or change another company's rows, whatever the screen asks for. We test this automatically.

Role-based access

Drivers see their own jobs and hours. Warehouse staff see stock. Only admins manage members, billing and data import.

Encrypted

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest by our hosting provider. Passwords are never stored in readable form.

Audit trail

Sign-ins, role changes, AI decisions and data changes are written to an append-only log. Nobody can edit or delete entries, including admins.

UK GDPR paperwork

A privacy policy, data processing agreement and terms are available. Admins can export vehicles, jobs and stock as CSV at any time.

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. The app stores only your sign-in session on your device. See the cookie notice.

FAQ

Questions operators ask.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Your data is kept. The workspace becomes read-only until an admin chooses a plan in Admin, then Billing. Nothing is charged automatically, because the trial never asks for a card.

Do drivers need to install an app?

No. Northline runs in the phone's web browser on iPhone and Android. Drivers can add it to their home screen so it opens like an app.

Can we bring in our existing data?

Yes. Admins can import vehicles, jobs and stock from CSV files exported from Excel or Numbers. The onboarding fee covers us doing this with you.

Does it connect to our telematics or tachograph system?

Integrations are built per customer on the Enterprise plan. Tell us which systems you use when you book a demo and we'll tell you honestly what's possible and how long it would take.

Will the AI make decisions on its own?

No. Models make proposals with a confidence score and a range. A person with the right role approves or rejects each one, and that decision is logged. The legal hours guard is locked on and cannot be switched off.

Where is our data stored, and who can see it?

In the London region. Only your own staff, with the roles you give them, can see your company's data. Our platform team can see your company name, plan, vehicle and member counts and admin email addresses for billing and support, but not your operational records.

Is Northline legal advice?

No. The drivers' hours and compliance features help you follow the rules, but you remain responsible for meeting them. Always check current guidance on GOV.UK and from the DVSA.

Can we cancel?

Yes, at any time from the billing portal in Admin, then Billing. Admins can export vehicles, jobs and stock as CSV files first.
