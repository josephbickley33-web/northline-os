2. Processor obligations

  • Process only on the Customer's documented instructions (these terms and the Customer's use of the service).
  • Ensure people authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality.
  • Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (Annex 2).
  • Use sub-processors only as listed (Annex 3), with equivalent written terms, and give [30] days' notice of changes so the Customer can object.
  • Assist with data subject requests, security, breach notification, DPIAs and prior consultation, taking into account the nature of processing.
  • Notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within [48] hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach.
  • At the end of the service, delete or return the data at the Customer's choice within [30] days, unless law requires storage.
  • Make available information needed to demonstrate compliance and allow audits [on reasonable notice, at most once a year, at the cost of the Customer].

3. International transfers

Customer data is stored in the London region (eu-west-2). Any restricted transfer (for example, sub-processor support access) will be covered by [the UK IDTA, UK Addendum or UK–US Data Bridge].

Annex 2: Security measures

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest (hosting provider).
  • Per-customer isolation enforced by database row-level security, covered by automated tests.
  • Role-based access for Customer users; platform staff do not see operational records through the product.
  • Append-only audit log of sign-ins, role changes, AI decisions and data changes.
  • Private file storage for photos and signatures, segregated by customer.
  • [Backups, restore testing, staff access, MFA for admin accounts, vulnerability management, incident response.]
