The website

The Northline website (the home page, pricing and these legal pages) sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tools. Fonts and scripts are served from our own site, so visiting does not send your details to font or analytics companies.

If we ever add analytics, we will use a cookie-free, privacy-friendly tool that does not track individuals across sites (for example a self-hosted or EU/UK-hosted, cookieless service), update this notice first, and ask for consent where the law requires it.

The Northline app

When you sign in, the app stores your sign-in session (an access token and refresh token) in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It may also remember an invite link you opened until you finish joining. These are strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so they do not need consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). Signing out removes the session.
